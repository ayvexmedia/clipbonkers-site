Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy covers the clipbonkers uploader ("the app"), a private internal tool that the clipbonkers team uses to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel, @clipbonkers.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • An OAuth 2.0 access token and refresh token for the clipbonkers YouTube channel, granted by the channel owner when signing in.
  • The channel's name and ID, read once to confirm the app is signed in to the right channel.
  • The video files, titles and descriptions we upload, which are our own content.

The app does not access, collect or store any data about other YouTube users, viewers or channels.

How the data is used

The tokens are used only to upload our own videos to our own channel and to confirm which channel is signed in. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or analytics about any person.

Storage and security

The tokens are stored only on the clipbonkers team's own computer, in a file that is excluded from any backups or code repositories. They are never sent anywhere except to Google's servers when the app calls YouTube API Services.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data obtained through YouTube API Services with anyone.

Cookies and tracking

The app is a desktop tool. It sets no cookies and does not track or identify the devices of any user. This website sets no cookies.

Retention and deletion

The tokens are kept only while the app is in use. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page; once revoked, the tokens stop working and are deleted from our computer. To ask us to delete any data, email ayvexmedia@gmail.com; we will delete it within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
