clipbonkers is a clip channel that posts short highlights from live streams on YouTube (@clipbonkers), TikTok and X.

The clipbonkers uploader

The clipbonkers uploader is a private, internal tool. It is used only by the clipbonkers team to upload our own Shorts to our own YouTube channel, @clipbonkers. It is not offered to other users, and nobody outside the team can sign in to it.

The uploader uses YouTube API Services to upload videos (youtube.videos.insert) and to confirm which channel it is signed in to (youtube.channels.list). It does not read, store or share data from any other YouTube account.

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
